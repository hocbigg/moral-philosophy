Great Courses in Moral Philosophy

A curated directory of high-signal, full-length university lecture series, OpenCourseWare collections, and specialized online courses in Moral Philosophy. These audiovisual resources build deep theoretical intuition, demonstrate dialectical argumentation, and bridge abstract ethics with real-world dilemmas.

Normative Ethical Theory and Moral Reasoning

  • Justice: What's the Right Thing to Do? (Harvard University / Michael Sandel)
  • A Romp Through Ethics for Complete Beginners (University of Oxford Podcasts / Marianne Talbot)
  • General Philosophy (University of Oxford Podcasts / Peter Millican)

Metaethics and Moral Epistemology

  • Relativism (University of California, Irvine / Coursera - Duncan Pritchard)

Moral Psychology and Human Agency

  • Philosophy and the Science of Human Nature (Open Yale Courses / Tamar Gendler)
  • Death (Open Yale Courses / Shelly Kagan)

Political Philosophy and Distributive Justice

  • Moral Foundations of Politics (Yale University / Coursera - Ian Shapiro)
  • Introduction to Political Philosophy (Open Yale Courses / Steven B. Smith)

Applied, Biomedical, and Technology Ethics

  • Introduction to Bioethics (Georgetown University / edX - Kennedy Institute of Ethics)
  • Effective Altruism (Princeton University / Coursera - Peter Singer)
  • Ethics of AI (University of Helsinki / MOOC.fi)
  • Practical Ethics Bites (University of Oxford Podcasts / David Edmonds & Nigel Warburton)

Comparative and Asian Ethical Traditions

  • The Path to Happiness: What Chinese Philosophy Teaches Us About the Good Life (Harvard University / Michael Puett)
  • Humanity and Nature in Chinese Thought (University of Hong Kong / edX - Chad Hansen)
  • Buddhism and Modern Psychology (Princeton University / Coursera - Robert Wright)
