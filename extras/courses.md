# Great Courses

A curated directory of high-signal, full-length university lecture series, OpenCourseWare collections, and specialized online courses in **Moral Philosophy**. These audiovisual resources build deep theoretical intuition, demonstrate dialectical argumentation, and bridge abstract ethics with real-world dilemmas.

## Normative Frameworks, Metaethics & Moral Psychology

- [Ethics (MIT OpenCourseWare / Julia Markovits)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/24-231-ethics-fall-2009/)
- [Philosophy and the Science of Human Nature (Open Yale Courses / Tamar Gendler)](https://oyc.yale.edu/philosophy/phil-181)
- [A Romp Through Ethics for Complete Beginners (University of Oxford Podcasts / Marianne Talbot)](https://podcasts.ox.ac.uk/series/romp-through-ethics-complete-beginners)

## Political Philosophy, Justice & Social Choice

- [Justice: What's the Right Thing to Do? (Harvard University / Michael Sandel)](https://justiceharvard.org/)
- [Moral Foundations of Politics (Yale University / Coursera - Ian Shapiro)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/moral-politics)
- [Introduction to Political Philosophy (Open Yale Courses / Steven B. Smith)](https://oyc.yale.edu/political-science/plsc-114)

## Applied, Biomedical & Technology Ethics

- [Introduction to Bioethics (Georgetown University / edX)](https://www.edx.org/learn/ethics/georgetown-university-introduction-to-bioethics)
- [Effective Altruism (Princeton University / Coursera - Peter Singer)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/effective-altruism)
- [Ethics of AI (University of Helsinki / MOOC.fi)](https://ethics-of-ai.mooc.fi/)

- [Death (Open Yale Courses / Shelly Kagan)](https://oyc.yale.edu/death/phil-176)
