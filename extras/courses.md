# Great Courses in Moral Philosophy

A curated directory of high-signal, full-length university lecture series, OpenCourseWare collections, and specialized online courses in **Moral Philosophy**. These audiovisual resources build deep theoretical intuition, demonstrate dialectical argumentation, and bridge abstract ethics with real-world dilemmas.

## Normative Ethical Theory and Moral Reasoning

- [Justice: What's the Right Thing to Do? (Harvard University / Michael Sandel)](https://justiceharvard.org/)
- [A Romp Through Ethics for Complete Beginners (University of Oxford Podcasts / Marianne Talbot)](https://podcasts.ox.ac.uk/series/romp-through-ethics-complete-beginners)
- [General Philosophy (University of Oxford Podcasts / Peter Millican)](https://podcasts.ox.ac.uk/series/general-philosophy-2018)

## Metaethics and Moral Epistemology

- [Relativism (University of California, Irvine / Coursera - Duncan Pritchard)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/relativism)

## Moral Psychology and Human Agency

- [Philosophy and the Science of Human Nature (Open Yale Courses / Tamar Gendler)](https://oyc.yale.edu/philosophy/phil-181)
- [Death (Open Yale Courses / Shelly Kagan)](https://oyc.yale.edu/philosophy/phil-176)

## Political Philosophy and Distributive Justice

- [Moral Foundations of Politics (Yale University / Coursera - Ian Shapiro)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/moral-politics)
- [Introduction to Political Philosophy (Open Yale Courses / Steven B. Smith)](https://oyc.yale.edu/political-science/plsc-114)

## Applied, Biomedical, and Technology Ethics

- [Introduction to Bioethics (Georgetown University / edX - Kennedy Institute of Ethics)](https://www.edx.org/learn/ethics/georgetown-university-introduction-to-bioethics)
- [Effective Altruism (Princeton University / Coursera - Peter Singer)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/effective-altruism)
- [Ethics of AI (University of Helsinki / MOOC.fi)](https://ethics-of-ai.mooc.fi/)
- [Practical Ethics Bites (University of Oxford Podcasts / David Edmonds & Nigel Warburton)](https://podcasts.ox.ac.uk/series/practical-ethics-bites)

## Comparative and Asian Ethical Traditions

- [The Path to Happiness: What Chinese Philosophy Teaches Us About the Good Life (Harvard University / Michael Puett)](https://pll.harvard.edu/course/path-happiness-what-chinese-philosophy-teaches-us-about-good-life)
- [Humanity and Nature in Chinese Thought (University of Hong Kong / edX - Chad Hansen)](https://www.edx.org/learn/philosophy/the-university-of-hong-kong-humanity-and-nature-in-chinese-thought)
- [Buddhism and Modern Psychology (Princeton University / Coursera - Robert Wright)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/science-of-meditation)
